## Notes for Outreach

- **Best email-discovery move:** for any row above, visit the site's `/contact` page — most list a real address that beats the info@ guess.
- **Chamber directory** (wallerchamber.com/list) is a goldmine — every member there has a profile page with verified contact info, often including a direct email. Worth pulling 20–30 more from the alpha-search pages (`/list/searchalpha/a` … `/z`).
- **For ConvoPro pitch fit:** restaurants, dental, pest control, HVAC, childcare, and event venues are highest-intent for SMS/voice AI — high inbound call volume, after-hours leakage, scheduling pain.
- **Verify before sending guessed emails** — bounces hurt sender reputation. Tools like Hunter.io or NeverBounce can validate in bulk.
